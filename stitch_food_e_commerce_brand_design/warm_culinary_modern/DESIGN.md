## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a tactile, appetizing, and conversion-focused digital storefront tailored for fast-casual food ordering and gourmet e-commerce. Built mobile-first, it evokes trust, freshness, prompt delivery, and culinary warmth. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Modern Functional Minimalism** with high-clarity transactional hierarchy:
- **60-30-10 Distribution Rule**: 60% neutral framework (soft gray canvas with crisp borders), 30% clean pure white card surfaces that allow rich food photography to shine, and a calibrated 10% intentional accent punch (Orange-700) reserved strictly for calls-to-action, active navigational states, and critical transactional anchors.
- **Audience & Context**: Built for urban food delivery and checkout flow in high-velocity mobile settings, with specialized numeric readability for Nigerian Naira (`₦`) pricing and tabular operational states.

## Colors

The color system enforces strict proportion and semantic intent:

- **Dominant Base (60%)**: `page-bg` (`#F9FAFB` / `gray-50`) forms the continuous canvas, framed with crisp hairline borders (`#F3F4F6` and `#E5E7EB`).
- **Elevated Canvas (30%)**: Clean `#FFFFFF` cards, drawers, and sheets isolate menu items, ingredient disclosures, and receipt lines with high contrast against the canvas.
- **Brand Action (10%)**: `orange-700` (`#C2410C`) drives conversion—used for primary action buttons, selected radio nodes, active tab indicators, and cart badge tallies. `orange-50` provides tinted selection states.
- **Promotional Accent**: `amber-100` (`#FEF3C7`) serves exclusively as the background pill for discount tags, seasonal specials, and meal combo badges, paired with `amber-800`.
- **Fulfillment & Order Lifecyle Status Tokens**:
  - `emerald-600` (`#059669`): Paid, completed, delivered, fresh items.
  - `amber-500` (`#F59E0B`): Pending payment, kitchen preparation, queued orders.
  - `red-600` (`#DC2626`): Cancelled, stock depleted, validation error alerts.
  - `sky-500` (`#0EA5E9`): Courier dispatched, out for delivery, GPS transit tracking.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, warm food titles and section headers with **Inter** for dense transactional elements, descriptions, and checkout specifications.

- **Tabular Figures Requirement**: All price tokens (`price-lg`, `price-md`, `price-sm`) and quantity indicators must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` / `tabular-nums`. This prevents numeric jitter during dynamic cart updates, total recalculations, and delivery fee tallies in Nigerian Naira (`₦`).
- **Naira Currency Handling**: The `₦` currency symbol inherits the weight and tabular spacing of the adjacent numeral without artificial superscripting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first on a 4-based Tailwind scale:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px)**: Single column fluid stack. Outer canvas margin is `1rem` (`16px`), gutter is `1rem` (`16px`). Fixed sticky bottom navigation and action sheets span full width minus safe areas.
- **Tablet Viewport (640px - 1023px)**: 2-column dish catalogue grid. Outer margins scale to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Desktop Viewport (1024px+)**: Max container width of `1280px` centered with auto margins. 3 or 4-column product grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Checkout flow shifts to an asymmetric 7:5 split (7-column order steps, 5-column sticky cart summary).
- **Component Padding Rhythm**:
  - Tight containers, inputs, and chips utilize `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Cards, modals, and list items maintain internal breathing room via `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy dropshadows to preserve a fresh, hygienic e-commerce presentation:

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: `gray-50` (`#F9FAFB`) flat surface.
- **Card & Container Elevation (`shadow-sm`)**: Pure white containers use `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` coupled with a continuous subtle border stroke of `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Interactive Hover & Drag Elevation (`shadow-md`)**: Active food cards, hover states, and quantity steppers transition smoothly to `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Cart Bars**: `shadow-lg` (`0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) with `backdrop-blur-md` and `bg-white/95` background treatment for sticky mobile action sheets.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes clear geometrical roles:

- **`rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`)**: Applied universally to card containers, product photo bounding boxes, category tiles, bottom sheets, and promotional modals.
- **`rounded-md` (6px / `0.375rem`)**: Strictly applied to operational UI controls—primary/secondary buttons, input fields, dropdown toggles, and quantity adjustment counters.
- **`rounded-full` (9999px)**: Applied exclusively to status tags, category pills, discount badges, avatar containers, and numeric notification bubbles.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, Checkout, Pay Now)**: `bg-orange-700 text-white font-medium text-sm py-2.5 px-4 rounded-md shadow-sm hover:bg-orange-800 active:scale-[0.98] transition-all`. Focus ring: `ring-2 ring-orange-700/30 ring-offset-2`.
- **Secondary (Customise, Reorder, View Details)**: `bg-white text-gray-800 border border-gray-200 font-medium text-sm py-2.5 px-4 rounded-md shadow-sm hover:bg-gray-50 active:bg-gray-100`.
- **Destructive (Clear Cart, Cancel Order)**: `bg-red-50 text-red-600 border border-red-200 font-medium text-sm py-2.5 px-4 rounded-md hover:bg-red-100`.

### 2. Product & Food Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `bg-white border border-gray-200 rounded-lg overflow-hidden shadow-sm flex flex-col`.
- **Media**: 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratio container with `bg-gray-100` fallback placeholder, food image with `object-cover`.
- **Body**: Internal padding `p-4 flex flex-col justify-between flex-1`.
- **Price & Buy**: Price rendered in `font-bold text-gray-900 tabular-nums` paired with an icon or compact text button aligned to the right.

### 3. Chips & Category Filters
- **Default State**: `bg-white text-gray-700 border border-gray-200 rounded-full px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-medium hover:border-gray-300 transition-colors`.
- **Selected State**: `bg-orange-700 text-white border-transparent rounded-full px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-semibold shadow-sm`.

### 4. Status Badges & Promo Tags
- **Promo Tag**: `bg-amber-100 text-amber-800 font-semibold text-xs px-2.5 py-0.5 rounded-full inline-flex items-center gap-1`.
- **Status Indicators (Pills)**:
  - *Delivered / Paid*: `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border border-emerald-200/60 rounded-full text-xs font-medium px-2 py-0.5`.
  - *Preparing*: `bg-amber-50 text-amber-700 border border-amber-200/60 rounded-full text-xs font-medium px-2 py-0.5`.
  - *Out for Delivery*: `bg-sky-50 text-sky-700 border border-sky-200/60 rounded-full text-xs font-medium px-2 py-0.5`.
  - *Cancelled*: `bg-red-50 text-red-700 border border-red-200/60 rounded-full text-xs font-medium px-2 py-0.5`.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs**: `bg-white border border-gray-300 rounded-md px-3.5 py-2 text-sm text-gray-900 placeholder:text-gray-400 focus:outline-none focus:border-orange-700 focus:ring-1 focus:ring-orange-700 transition-colors`.
- **Quantity Stepper**: Container `flex items-center border border-gray-200 rounded-md bg-white p-0.5`. Buttons `w-8 h-8 flex items-center justify-center text-gray-600 hover:bg-gray-100 active:bg-gray-200 rounded`. Center value `px-3 text-sm font-semibold tabular-nums text-gray-900`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `text-orange-700 border-gray-300 rounded focus:ring-orange-700 focus:ring-offset-0`.

### 6. Sticky Mobile Order Bar
- Fixed bottom panel: `fixed bottom-0 left-0 right-0 bg-white/95 backdrop-blur-md border-t border-gray-200 px-4 py-3 shadow-lg z-40 flex items-center justify-between`. Left shows summary count and `₦` price; right holds full primary CTA button.
